Professional Skill
Material Requirement Planning

Planning material requirements based on demand, inventory, open orders, lead times and production requirements.

Practical Application

Helps determine what materials are needed, how much is required and when replenishment should occur.

Key Areas

Inputs include demand, BOM requirements, current stock, scheduled receipts, lead time and planning parameters.

Relevant KPIs

KPIs include material availability, shortages, excess stock and planning accuracy.
