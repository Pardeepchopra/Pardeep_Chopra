Professional Skill
Job-Work Reconciliation

Reconciliation of materials issued to outside job workers against materials received back and related consumption or wastage.

Practical Application

Ensures material movement and job-work transactions remain traceable and balanced.

Key Areas

It involves tracking issues, receipts, process losses, returns, pending quantities and documentation.

Relevant KPIs

KPIs include pending job-work quantity, reconciliation variance and turnaround time.
