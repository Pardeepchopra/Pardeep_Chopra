Professional Skill

Material Management

Systematic planning and control of materials from requirement identification through procurement, receipt, storage and consumption.

Practical Application

Connects material availability with production requirements while controlling inventory investment.

Key Areas

Includes material classification, consumption analysis, planning parameters, stock monitoring and coordination with purchase and production.

Relevant KPIs

Useful KPIs include material availability, inventory turnover, excess stock, stock-outs and planning accuracy.
