Professional Skill
VED Analysis

Classifying inventory as Vital, Essential or Desirable according to operational criticality.

Practical Application

Helps prioritise control and availability of materials whose shortage can have different operational consequences.

Key Areas

Criticality assessment supports stocking policies, review frequency and availability priorities.

Relevant KPIs

Useful measures include critical-item availability, stock-outs and service levels by category.
