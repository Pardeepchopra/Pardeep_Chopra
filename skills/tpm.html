Professional Skill

TPM

Total Productive Maintenance, a structured approach to improving equipment effectiveness through proactive maintenance and involvement across functions.

Practical Application

Stores can support TPM through availability of critical spares, proper identification, preservation and controlled inventory.

Key Areas

Important practices include spare classification, critical-spare availability, preventive-maintenance support and inventory discipline.

Relevant KPIs

KPIs may include spare availability, equipment downtime and maintenance-related stock-outs.
