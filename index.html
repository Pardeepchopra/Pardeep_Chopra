Analytical Decision Support

From Inventory Data to Management Decisions

Practical analytical tools for replenishment, order quantity, inventory classification, stock movement, ageing and working-capital control.

Min-Max & Reorder Point

Determine minimum level, maximum level, reorder point and replenishment quantity from consumption, lead time and safety stock.

EOQ / Order Quantity

Calculate economical order quantity and understand the relationship between demand, ordering cost and carrying cost.

Inventory Turnover

Measure how efficiently inventory is converted through consumption or cost of goods and identify slow capital rotation.

ABC Analysis

Classify inventory according to annual consumption value and establish differentiated control priorities.

FSN Analysis

Identify Fast, Slow and Non-Moving materials from issue activity and support inventory rationalization.

Inventory Ageing

Analyse stock by age, identify ageing exposure and support action on excess, obsolete and dead inventory.
