Professional Skill

Ageing Analysis

Analysing inventory according to the length of time it has remained unused or unconsumed.

Practical Application

Highlights slow-moving and potentially obsolete stock requiring review or action.

Key Areas

Typical ageing buckets can be defined according to the organisation's requirements and material characteristics.

Relevant KPIs

KPIs include ageing value, ageing quantity, slow-moving percentage and reduction in old stock.
