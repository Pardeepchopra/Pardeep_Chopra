Material Issue & Return

Controlled issue of materials from Stores to authorized users and systematic return of unused, excess or rejected material with complete quantity, condition and transaction traceability.

Definition

Material issue is the authorized transfer of inventory from a controlled Stores location to a department, work order, cost centre, project or point of use. Material return is the controlled movement of unused, excess or otherwise returnable material back into Stores.

Issue-control principleAuthorize → Identify → Pick → Verify → Issue → Record → Acknowledge → Reconcile

Objectives

  • Provide the right material in the right quantity to the authorized user.
  • Prevent unauthorized withdrawal and inventory leakage.
  • Maintain accurate stock balances and consumption history.
  • Link material consumption to the correct department, work order, project or cost centre.
  • Protect material condition and traceability.
  • Recover unused or excess material where technically acceptable.

Issue Workflow

  1. Receive an approved material requisition or system demand.
  2. Verify item code, description, UOM, quantity and authorization.
  3. Check available and reserved stock.
  4. Select the correct batch, lot or serial according to issue policy.
  5. Pick and physically verify the material.
  6. Record the issue in ERP or approved transaction system.
  7. Obtain user acknowledgement where required.
  8. Update bin/location and stock balance.
  9. Review exceptions such as substitutions, shortages or emergency issues.

Return Workflow

  1. Receive return request with original issue reference.
  2. Verify identity and quantity.
  3. Inspect physical condition and usability.
  4. Determine whether material is reusable, requires inspection, quarantine or disposal.
  5. Return to the correct stock/status location.
  6. Record the return transaction against the original issue where practical.
  7. Reconcile physical and ERP balances.

FIFO / FEFO Application

Issue sequence should follow the applicable material-rotation rule. FEFO is particularly relevant where expiry or use-by dates control usability. FIFO is commonly used where receipt age is the principal rotation factor. Critical equipment spares may require a specific issue rule based on condition, compatibility and engineering policy.

Controls

  • No issue without authorized demand.
  • Separate physical picking from transaction confirmation where segregation of duties is required.
  • Verify high-value and critical items independently.
  • Maintain serial/batch traceability where applicable.
  • Investigate negative stock or repeated manual adjustments.
  • Review unreturned tools, containers and temporary issues.

Common Errors

  • Wrong item picked because of similar descriptions.
  • Issue recorded in the wrong UOM.
  • Material issued to the wrong cost centre or work order.
  • Physical issue completed without ERP posting.
  • Returns placed into usable stock without condition inspection.
  • Old stock bypassed while newer stock is issued.

KPIs

Issue Turnaround

Time from approved demand to issue completion.

Issue Accuracy

Correct item and quantity issued relative to total issues.

Return Rate

Value/quantity returned relative to issues.

Emergency Issue Rate

Emergency issues relative to total issues.

Excel / MIS Application

Track Issue No., Date, Item Code, Description, UOM, Bin, Quantity, Batch/Serial, Department, Work Order, Cost Centre, Requester, Issuer, Return Quantity, Return Date and Remarks.
