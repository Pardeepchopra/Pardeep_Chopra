Stores 5S & TPM

Application of 5S and Total Productive Maintenance principles to industrial Stores for workplace organization, visual control, equipment reliability, safety, cleanliness and disciplined operations.

Definition

5S provides a structured approach to workplace organization and visual discipline: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize and Sustain. TPM applies preventive and operator-involved maintenance principles to handling equipment, racks, material-handling systems and other Store assets.

Stores improvement principleRemove → Arrange → Clean → Standardize → Sustain → Maintain Equipment → Measure

5S in Stores

SStores Application
SortRemove obsolete, broken, duplicate and unnecessary items from working areas
Set in OrderAssign locations, labels, racks, bins and visual identification
ShineClean floors, racks, handling equipment and storage areas
StandardizeDefine layouts, labels, cleaning standards and inspection routines
SustainAudit, train, review and continuously improve the standard

TPM in Stores

  • Maintain forklifts, pallet trucks, cranes, hoists and other handling equipment.
  • Use preventive maintenance schedules.
  • Conduct operator checks before use.
  • Record breakdowns and downtime.
  • Control safety devices and load-rated equipment.
  • Analyze recurring failures.
  • Maintain critical spare parts for Store equipment.

Implementation Workflow

  1. Define Store zones and standards.
  2. Remove unnecessary materials and equipment.
  3. Create location and visual-management standards.
  4. Clean and inspect the workplace.
  5. Define standard work and checklists.
  6. Train Store personnel.
  7. Conduct regular 5S/TPM audits.
  8. Record abnormalities and corrective actions.
  9. Review KPI trends.
  10. Standardize successful improvements.

Visual Management

  • Bin/location labels.
  • Floor and aisle markings.
  • Maximum/minimum level indicators.
  • Quarantine and rejected-stock identification.
  • Fire and emergency markings.
  • Equipment status boards.
  • Cleaning and inspection schedules.
  • RGP/job-work outstanding boards.

Common Errors

  • Treating 5S as one-time cleaning.
  • Keeping obsolete materials in active storage.
  • Unlabelled temporary locations.
  • Blocked aisles and emergency access.
  • Skipping equipment inspections.
  • Closing abnormalities without corrective action.
