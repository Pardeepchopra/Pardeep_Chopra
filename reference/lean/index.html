PROFESSIONAL REFERENCE · LEAN & MRO

Lean & MRO

A practical reference for lean material flow and Maintenance, Repair & Operating inventory, connecting JIT, Kanban, pull systems, visual management, MRO criticality, spares, preservation, ageing and maintenance availability.

01

Lean Materials Management

Lean materials management aligns material flow, replenishment, inventory and information flow with actual operational demand while removing avoidable waste.

 02

Just-in-Time Materials Management

JIT seeks to make the required material available in the required quantity, at the required place and time, while minimizing unnecessary inventory and process waste.

 03

Kanban in Lean Materials Management

Kanban provides a visual pull signal that authorizes replenishment or production according to defined consumption, container and replenishment rules.

 04

Pull Material Flow

Pull replenishment responds to downstream consumption and uses actual demand signals to control upstream material movement.

 05

Push Material Flow

Push flow releases material based on plans, forecasts, schedules or predetermined supply decisions rather than only on downstream consumption.

 06

Push-Pull Hybrid in Lean

Hybrid material systems combine planned supply and pull signals where different stages of the material flow require different controls.

 07

Supermarket System

A material supermarket maintains controlled replenishment stock from which downstream users withdraw material and generate replenishment signals.

 08

Milk Run Material Replenishment

Milk-run logistics uses scheduled routes and collection or delivery cycles to replenish multiple material points efficiently.

 09

Takt Time and Materials

Takt time provides a demand-based rhythm that can help determine replenishment frequency and material-flow requirements.

 10

Heijunka and Material Flow

Heijunka levels production demand to reduce abrupt changes that create instability in material requirements and replenishment.

 11

Value Stream Mapping for Materials

Value Stream Mapping examines material and information flow from supply through production to identify delay, excess inventory and other waste.

 12

Seven Wastes in Materials Management

Lean materials management addresses transportation, inventory, motion, waiting, overproduction, over-processing and defects as sources of operational waste.

 13

Visual Management for Materials

Visual management makes stock status, replenishment signals, locations, shortages and exceptions immediately understandable.

 14

Standard Work for Materials Management

Standard work defines the agreed sequence, timing, quantity, responsibility and control points for recurring material activities.

 15

Kaizen in Materials Management

Kaizen uses structured incremental improvement to reduce material-flow problems, inventory waste, handling effort and process variation.

 16

5S for Stores and Materials

5S organizes, cleans, standardizes and sustains the workplace so material identification, access, preservation and visual control improve.

 17

TPM and Materials Management

Total Productive Maintenance influences spare-parts availability, maintenance stores, equipment reliability and material readiness.

 18

MRO Inventory Fundamentals

MRO inventory covers maintenance, repair and operating materials required to maintain plant, equipment and operational continuity.

 19

MRO Material Classification

MRO items can be classified by equipment function, criticality, demand pattern, value, lead time and replacement characteristics.

 20

Mechanical MRO Spares

Mechanical MRO includes bearings, seals, couplings, gears, belts, chains, pumps, valves and other equipment-related parts.

 21

Electrical MRO Spares

Electrical MRO includes motors, contactors, relays, breakers, cables, fuses, switches and other electrical maintenance materials.

 22

Instrumentation MRO Spares

Instrumentation MRO includes sensors, transmitters, controllers, indicators, control components and related instrumentation parts.

 23

Bearings Inventory Management

Bearing inventory requires correct identification, storage preservation, condition control, demand review and criticality-based stocking.

 24

Motors and Pumps Inventory

Motors and pumps require equipment-specific identification, criticality assessment, preservation and planned spare availability.

 25

Valves Inventory Management

Valve control should consider size, rating, material, service application, end connection, interchangeability and equipment criticality.

 26

Belts and Lubricants Inventory

Belts and lubricants require specification control, storage conditions, shelf-life or condition monitoring and appropriate replenishment.

 27

Fasteners and Welding Consumables

Fasteners and welding consumables require standardized specifications, UOM discipline, controlled bins and consumption-based replenishment.

 28

Industrial Chemicals as MRO Materials

Industrial chemicals require specification, compatibility, preservation, controlled issue and applicable safety and regulatory controls.

 29

MRO Criticality Analysis

MRO criticality considers equipment consequence, failure impact, lead time, availability, redundancy and maintenance strategy when determining stock policy.

 30

Insurance / Critical Spares

Insurance spares protect against high-consequence equipment failures where replacement lead time or operational impact justifies strategic availability.

 31

Routine MRO Spares

Routine spares are regularly consumed or replaced and can often use structured consumption, lead-time and replenishment controls.

 32

Intermittent Demand in MRO

MRO demand is often intermittent or sporadic, requiring methods that recognize long zero-demand periods and occasional high requirements.

 33

MRO Obsolescence Management

MRO obsolescence control addresses obsolete equipment, discontinued components, technology changes, duplicate stock and unusable spare parts.

 34

Shutdown & Maintenance Inventory

Shutdown planning consolidates maintenance requirements, checks availability and creates controlled material readiness before the outage.

 35

MRO Lead Time Management

MRO lead-time control combines supplier lead time, repair turnaround, inspection and internal processing to determine practical replenishment protection.

 36

Repairable / Rotable Spares

Repairable and rotable spares require return, repair, inspection, certification, availability and cycle tracking rather than simple one-way consumption.

 37

MRO Vendor Management

MRO supplier management focuses on availability, technical conformity, emergency response, repair capability, lead time and lifecycle support.

 38

MRO Spare Preservation

Preservation protects spare condition through appropriate packaging, environmental control, rotation, inspection and storage discipline.

 39

MRO Min-Max Replenishment

Min-Max can control routine MRO materials by linking consumption, lead time, safety stock and practical replenishment quantities.

 40

MRO Kanban

Kanban can be applied to predictable, frequently consumed MRO items where standard quantities, locations and replenishment cycles are practical.

 41

MRO Inventory Ageing

Ageing analysis identifies long-held MRO stock and supports review of relevance, equipment status, preservation, transfer, return or disposal.

 42

MRO Dead Inventory

Dead MRO inventory has no current movement or identified requirement and requires structured review before being classified as obsolete or disposable.

 43

MRO Working Capital Control

MRO working-capital control balances plant availability against the financial exposure created by excess, duplicate and obsolete spares.

 44

MRO Stores and ERP

ERP supports MRO material identity, equipment linkage, stock status, reservations, issues, receipts, repair cycles and reporting.

 45

MRO Inventory KPIs

MRO performance can be measured through availability, stockout events, critical-spare coverage, ageing, excess, repair turnaround and inventory value.

 46

Lean and MRO Integration

Lean principles and MRO controls must be balanced so inventory reduction does not create unacceptable equipment-availability risk.

 47

Lean & MRO Governance

Governance defines ownership, classification, parameter review, criticality approval, master-data standards, exception escalation and audit controls.

 48

Industrial Lean & MRO Case Framework

A practical framework for selecting lean flow methods and MRO inventory controls across stores, maintenance and production environments.
