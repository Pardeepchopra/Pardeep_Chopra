Inventory Ageing

Practical reference for inventory ageing, age buckets, dormant stock identification and management action.

1. Inventory Ageing

Inventory ageing measures how long inventory has remained without the required level of movement or utilization, using a defined ageing basis.

Ageing analysis is particularly useful for identifying dormant, slow-moving, excess and potentially obsolete materials.

2. Ageing Basis

Common starting points include:

  • Date of last issue.
  • Date of last consumption.
  • Date of receipt.
  • Date of last movement.
  • Date inventory became excess.
The chosen basis must match the business question. For example, ageing based on last issue is useful for identifying consumption inactivity, while receipt age can be useful for identifying old stock physically held for a long period.

3. Example Age Buckets

Age BucketPossible InterpretationReview Action
0–30 daysRecently received or moved.Normal monitoring.
31–90 daysModerately aged.Review expected consumption.
91–180 daysIncreasing inactivity.Check requirement and planning parameters.
181–365 daysOld stock.Detailed review.
Above 365 daysPotentially dormant/obsolete depending on item characteristics.Management action and requirement validation.

These buckets are examples. The actual policy should reflect material type, industry, seasonality, criticality and expected usage.

4. Ageing Calculation

Age in Days = Analysis Date − Relevant Reference Date

The reference date may be last issue date, last movement date, receipt date or another formally defined date.

6. Management Actions for Aged Inventory

  1. Validate whether future consumption exists.
  2. Check open maintenance or production requirements.
  3. Check whether equivalent items exist under another code.
  4. Review excess quantities.
  5. Explore transfer to another department or plant where appropriate.
  6. Consider return to supplier where contractually possible.
  7. Consider alternate application.
  8. Identify obsolete material according to policy.
  9. Initiate approved disposal where justified.

7. Ageing Dashboard KPIs

Total Aged Stock ValueValue of stock beyond the selected ageing threshold.
Aged Stock %Aged stock value as a proportion of total inventory value.
Above 365 DaysValue or quantity exceeding the defined long-age threshold.
Ageing ReductionReduction in aged stock over a selected period.
