Stock Coverage & Inventory Days

Days or weeks of demand supported by available inventory.

Definition

Days or weeks of demand supported by available inventory. This reference is designed for practical industrial stores, MRO, production and materials-management environments.

Objective

Express current inventory in time-based demand coverage.

Scope

Apply the method at item, category, location or plant level as appropriate. The control should connect planning, purchasing, stores transactions, physical verification and management review.

Required Inputs

  • Usable stock
  • average daily consumption
  • demand forecast.

Methodology

  1. Define the business requirement, scope and review period.
  2. Validate item master, consumption, stock and open transaction data.
  3. Calculate or classify using the defined method.
  4. Compare the result with criticality, supplier constraints and future demand.
  5. Assign an action owner, due date and review frequency.
  6. Monitor KPI movement and revise parameters when conditions change.

Formula / Control Logic

Stock Coverage Days = Usable Inventory ÷ Average Daily Consumption.

Worked Industrial Example

Usable stock 6,000 units and average daily consumption 250 → 24 days coverage.

Decision Rules

  • Use usable stock, excluding blocked/quarantine where appropriate.
  • Use forward demand for seasonal businesses.
  • Compare coverage with lead time and service requirement.

Industrial Controls

  • Authorized master-data and parameter changes
  • ERP transaction discipline and document traceability
  • Physical verification and reconciliation
  • Exception reporting with named ownership
  • Periodic management review of ageing, service and capital exposure

KPIs

Service

Availability, fill rate and stock-out performance.

Accuracy

Book-to-physical reliability and transaction quality.

Capital

Turnover, coverage, ageing, excess and dead stock.

Common Errors

  • Using closing stock without considering commitments.
  • Comparing against outdated consumption.
  • Treating more days as always better.

Excel / MIS Application

Useful fields: Item Code, Description, UOM, Opening Qty, Receipt Qty, Issue Qty, Closing Qty, Unit Rate, Annual Consumption, Current Stock, Open PO, Lead Time, Safety Stock, ROP, Min, Max, ROQ, ABC, VED, FSN, Ageing, Action.

Management Review Questions

  • Which items require action now, and why?
  • Are open orders aligned with actual requirement?
  • Which exceptions are recurring and what is the root cause?
  • Which parameter should be changed, and what evidence supports the change?
