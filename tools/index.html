Industrial Materials Management Tools

Tools convert defined inputs into repeatable calculations and analysis. They are decision-support aids, not substitutes for professional judgement.

Purpose & Need

Tools convert defined inputs into repeatable calculations and analysis. They are decision-support aids, not substitutes for professional judgement.

Working Method

Use the calculators with controlled data, stated units and documented assumptions; validate unusual results against operational conditions and policy.

Professional Responsibility

A typical workflow is inputs → validation → calculation → interpretation → decision → review.

Governance & Quality

Tool governance requires clear formulas, units, version control and appropriate responsibility for the resulting decision.

Practical application
