Tool Development Principle

Each analytical tool will clearly show its calculation method, required inputs, assumptions, results and industrial interpretation.

The tools are intended to support professional analysis and decision-making. They do not replace an organization's approved inventory policy, ERP configuration, financial controls or management authorization.
