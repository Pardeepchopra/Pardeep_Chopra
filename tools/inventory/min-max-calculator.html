Calculation Method

Lead-Time Demand = Average Daily Consumption × Average Lead Time

ROP = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

Minimum Level = ROP

Maximum Level = Minimum Level + Reorder Quantity

This calculator uses a practical control method. Organizations may use different definitions of minimum level, maximum level, inventory position, safety stock and replenishment quantity depending on their ERP configuration and inventory policy.

Stock Status Logic

Current Stock Interpretation
Below Minimum Potential shortage / urgent replenishment review.
At or below ROP Replenishment trigger should be reviewed.
Between ROP and Maximum Normal operating range.
Above Maximum Potential excess stock; investigate before further purchase.

Industrial Application

This calculator can be used as a preliminary planning tool for MRO items, consumables, stores materials and other inventory where practical Min-Max control is appropriate.

For critical materials, statistical demand analysis, supplier reliability, open purchase orders, reserved quantities, service requirements and criticality should also be considered.
