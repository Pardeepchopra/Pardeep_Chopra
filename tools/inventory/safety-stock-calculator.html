Safety Stock Calculator

Estimate buffer stock from demand variability and lead-time conditions.

Professional Methodology

Safety Stock = (Maximum Daily Demand × Maximum Lead Time) − (Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time). ROP = Average Daily Demand × Average Lead Time + Safety Stock.

Professional Control Notes

  • Use consistent units and clearly defined time periods.
  • Record the source and date of every input.
  • Validate assumptions before using the result for inventory or purchasing decisions.
  • Review results against service level, criticality, lead time and operational constraints.
