Industrial Case Studies

Case studies connect principles to realistic plant situations and show how a decision develops from data to action.

Purpose & Need

Case studies connect principles to realistic plant situations and show how a decision develops from data to action.

Working Method

They help professionals understand trade-offs such as service versus inventory, price versus total cost and standardization versus criticality.

Professional Responsibility

Use a case method of situation → data → analysis → alternatives → decision → implementation → result → lesson.

Governance & Quality

Cases should distinguish facts, assumptions and managerial judgement.
