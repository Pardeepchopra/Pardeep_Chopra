Min-Max Planning Case Study

Practical case structure using data, methodology, controls, action ownership and KPI review.

Method

  1. Define the problem and business impact.
  2. Collect reliable transaction and master data.
  3. Analyze and segment the population.
  4. Calculate the relevant control parameters.
  5. Implement action with ownership and due dates.
  6. Review KPI movement and repeat exceptions.

Outputs

  • Exception list
  • Action owner
  • Impact estimate
  • Updated control parameter
  • Follow-up KPI
